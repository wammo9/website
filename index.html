About Me

I'm an Electrical & Computer Engineering and Computer Science undergraduate at CU Boulder with a deep interest in embedded software, where code meets real hardware. I'm currently the Software GUI Lead and Customer Relations Manager on a NASA SpaceGrant capstone building a distributed sensing platform, and I serve as Head Fellow for CU Engineering Fellows.

Outside of engineering, I'm a Nintendo and mobile-game fan (playing and building them), I teach calculus and engineering courses, train judo in the evenings, and recharge by reading and spending time with my Golden Retriever, Ginger.
