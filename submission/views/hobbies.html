Interests

Embedded Software

Embedded software combines hardware and software for specialized tasks in devices like smartwatches and cars. People use these devices every day, which is exactly the kind of impact I want to make.

Medicine

With a passion for helping people, medicine is always in the back of my mind. There are many places where medicine and technology combine to improve lives, and I'm eager to be part of that.

Judo

I'm an avid participant in Japanese culture (art, language, animation, and sport), and judo ties all of these together. It teaches patience, determination, and defense, traits I practice in everyday life.

Irish Dance

Irish dance lets me celebrate my heritage. Legend says dancers keep their arms down to hide their dance from those who tried to suppress Irish culture, a determination I try to carry into my own life.
